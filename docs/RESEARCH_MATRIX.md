## 2. Key Synthesis & Gaps Identified

1. **Gap 1: Disconnected Predictive Models & Optimization Genomes**  
   Most literature evaluates ML fuel models in isolation (e.g. Yan et al., Xiao et al.) without feeding them into combinatorial multi-objective optimizers. Q-GREEN bridges this with the **surrogate layer** (>20,000 lookups/s), preventing optimizer stall.

2. **Gap 2: Neglect of Upstream & Fugitive Slip Emissions**  
   Standard maritime routing assumes fossil MDO or treats alternative fuels as 100% clean. Under IMO MEPC.391(81), methane slip (GWP=28) and upstream synthesis penalties drastically alter true Pareto frontiers. Q-GREEN enforces strict pathway-level LCA.

3. **Gap 3: Fragile Claims of Quantum Supremacy**  
   Literature occasionally proposes toy 5-node D-Wave or QAOA models that cannot scale to realistic fleets with draft and tank constraints. Q-GREEN maintains strict intellectual honesty: **classical quantum-inspired heuristics only** running on standard CPUs.
