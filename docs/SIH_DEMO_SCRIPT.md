# Q-GREEN INDIA: SIH Demo Script

**Total Time Target:** 3 minutes

## Scene 1: The India-First Problem
*Show: INDIA GREEN MARITIME COMMAND CENTER (Screen 1)*
**Script:**
"Judges, maritime shipping accounts for nearly 3% of global greenhouse gas emissions. In India, as we expand our cargo flows through initiatives like Sagarmala and the Green Tug Transition Programme, operators face an impossible math problem: How to balance new alternative fuel costs, strict emissions compliance, and tight delivery schedules under monsoon uncertainty? Traditional routing software fails here. That's why we built **Q-GREEN INDIA**."

## Scene 2: The Digital Twin
*Show: INDIA DIGITAL TWIN (Screen 2)*
*Action: Select a route from Deendayal to JNPA for a 25,000t cargo shipment with a strict deadline.*
**Script:**
"This is our geographic Digital Twin of the Indian coastline. It maps real-world constraints—shipping lanes, standard port draft limits, Emission Control Areas, and ports equipped with Cold-Ironing (shore power). We don't use arbitrary distances; our model understands the actual maritime graph."

## Scene 3: The Intelligence Layer
*Show: Fuel prediction, ETA distribution, Weather*
**Script:**
"Before we optimize, our AI Trust Layer predicts the reality of the voyage. We fuse physics-informed ship hydrodynamic models with Machine Learning to predict fuel consumption and ETA distributions. It factors in current Indian monsoon regimes and port congestion probabilities, so we know exactly how risky a deadline is."

## Scene 4: The Optimizer Arena
*Show: OPTIMIZER ARENA (Screen 4)*
*Action: Run Q-GREEN vs NSGA-II vs ALNS*
**Script:**
"Here is where we solve the problem. We built a Classical Quantum-Inspired Search Engine. As you can see on the live convergence graph, our hybrid Q-GREEN algorithm searches millions of combinations of speeds, routes, and fuels—consistently beating standard industry algorithms in both speed and constraint feasibility."

## Scene 5: The Decision
*Show: PARETO DECISION EXPLORER (Screen 5)*
**Script:**
"Our engine doesn't just give one opaque answer. It generates a Pareto Front of optimal trade-offs. The operator can visually compare options: Plan A is cheapest, but Plan B cuts emissions by 20% for a marginal cost increase. The human remains in control."

## Scene 6: Why This Plan? (Explainability)
*Action: Click 'Why this plan?'*
**Script:**
"When a plan is selected, our Explainability Engine breaks down the 'Why'. It justifies the vessel choice, the fuel choice (e.g. Green Methanol), and pinpoints where the highest uncertainty lies."

## Scene 7: Shock & Disruption
*Show: STORM / DISRUPTION SIMULATOR (Screen 6)*
*Action: Trigger a Monsoon Storm + Port Congestion at JNPA.*
**Script:**
"The ocean is unpredictable. What happens if a severe monsoon cell hits the Arabian sea, and JNPA port backs up?"

## Scene 8: The Recovery
*Action: Re-optimize*
**Script:**
"Instead of resolving the entire global problem from scratch, our Local Model Predictive Control (MPC) paired with Q-GREEN re-optimizes instantly. It dynamically adjusts the vessel's speed envelope to make up for lost time without violating emissions rules."

## Scene 9: Strategic Scenario Engine
*Show: PORT TRANSITION READINESS (Screen 9)*
**Script:**
"Finally, Q-GREEN INDIA isn't just for daily operations. For government and port authorities, it serves as a Strategic Transition Simulator. What if we invest in Green Methanol bunkering at Cochin? What if we add Shore Power at Mundra? Our engine simulates how these infrastructure investments will affect fleet emissions and costs by 2030."

## Closing
"Q-GREEN INDIA is a production-ready, deterministic mathematical engine wrapped in an enterprise-grade UI. We don't fabricate data—every metric is derived from our pure-Python physics core. We give Indian fleet operators and policymakers the exact tools they need to achieve Net-Zero shipping. Thank you."
